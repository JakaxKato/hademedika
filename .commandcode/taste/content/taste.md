# Content

- Strongly opposes fabricated content: no fake/generated or stock photos of people, no invented doctor names, credentials, awards, testimonials, medical outcomes, availability, hours, prices, or review statistics. When information is unverified, prefers a clean editable placeholder or temporarily hiding the section. Confidence: 0.8
- Prefers cautious, honest customer-facing copy that avoids exaggerated marketing claims and states uncertainty plainly (e.g., bookings require admin confirmation, cost estimates are preliminary and not final). Confidence: 0.75
- Dislikes "apology"/unfinished placeholder copy in shipped UI; prefers placeholders that read as a deliberate temporary design state rather than an apology. Confidence: 0.65
